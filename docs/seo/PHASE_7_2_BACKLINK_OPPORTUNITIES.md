## 4. Backlink Quality & Anti-Spam Guardrails

To protect your portfolio from Google algorithmic penalties and maintain professional reputation:

1. **Zero Link Schemes:** No paid backlinks, sponsored links without `rel="sponsored"`, or private blog networks (PBNs).
2. **Zero Automated Submissions:** Submissions will be done manually, one platform at a time, adhering strictly to editorial formatting.
3. **No Low-Quality Web Directories:** Avoid generic free link directories, article farms, or unmoderated link aggregators.
4. **Contextual Relevance:** Every platform chosen must explicitly focus on software development, web engineering, open-source technology, or regional developer communities.
5. **Transparency & Accuracy:** Backlinks must reflect actual codebase features and real architecture (no inflated claims).
